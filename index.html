Some people break your trust. Some moments rebuild you.

2 September 2026

Jab Bharosa Tootta Hai

Ek waqt tha jab mujhe lagta tha ki kuch log kabhi nahi badlenge. Main un par bina soche bharosa karta tha, bina kisi shart ke, bina kisi doubt ke. Lekin waqt ne dheere-dheere samjha diya ki har insaan ko utni hi importance deni chahiye jitni woh aapko deta hai.

Jab hum kisi par poora bharosa karte hain, hum unhe apni zindagi ka ek hissa bana lete hain. Unki baaton mein, unke waadon mein, hum apna sukoon dhundhne lagte hain. Aur jab wahi insaan hume disappoint karta hai — chup ho jaata hai, ignore karta hai, ya bas ek din bina wajah door ho jaata hai — us waqt jo dard hota hai, woh sirf dard nahi hota. Woh ek sawaal ban jaata hai: maine kya galat kiya?

Trust once broken may be repaired, but it never returns exactly the way it was.

Sach yeh hai ki koi galti nahi hoti. Bas yeh samajhne mein waqt lagta hai ki sabka nazariya alag hota hai. Jis rishte ko aap sabse zyada value karte ho, zaroori nahi ki doosri taraf se bhi wahi feeling ho. Aur jab yeh realization hoti hai, sabse pehle jo aata hai woh hai — silence. Wahi silence jo har raat aapko akela feel karwata hai, jahan sawaal hote hain lekin jawaab nahi milte.

Main raaton ko sochta reh jaata tha — kya main itna bura tha? Kya meri koshishein kam thi? Overthinking ek aisa jaal hai jisme ek galat baat bhi sau tarah se ghumti rehti hai. Lekin sach yeh hai ki overthinking se sirf dard badhta hai, samaadhan nahi milta.

Dheere-dheere maine seekha ki har insaan se expect karna, apni khushi ko unke haath mein de dena hai. Aur jab yeh baat samajh aayi, tabhi se cheezein badalna shuru hui. Maine khud se poochna shuru kiya: agar main hi apni value nahi samjhunga, toh doosre kyun samjhenge?

Don't let someone's betrayal become the reason you stop believing in yourself.

Self-respect koi ghamand nahi hai. Yeh sirf itna hai ki aap apne aap ko utni hi respect dete ho jitni doosron ko dete ho. Jab kisi ne aapko chhod diya, ignore kar diya, ya aapki value nahi samjhi — uska matlab yeh nahi ki aap kam ho. Iska matlab sirf itna hai ki woh insaan aapke liye sahi jagah nahi tha.

Healing ek raat mein nahi hoti. Yeh ek dheemi, khamosh prakriya hai — kabhi ek achhi subah se shuru hoti hai, kabhi ek chhoti si khushi se. Aap achanak se theek nahi ho jaate, par har din thoda kam dukh hota hai, aur thoda zyada sukoon milta hai.

Jo log chale gaye, unse naaraz hona chhod do. Unse seekh lo aur aage badho. Jo chala gaya, usse lesson lo. Jo bacha hai, usse value karo — KHUD KO.

Pain sirf dard nahi hota — agar sahi tareeke se dekha jaaye, toh yeh ek teacher bhi ban sakta hai. Har disappointment ne mujhe kuch sikhaya: patience, boundaries, aur sabse zyada — apne aap par bharosa karna. Not everyone deserves the same level of trust you once gave so freely. That's not cynicism. That's growth.

Motivation isn't about forgetting the pain. It's about deciding that the pain won't decide who you become.

Aaj jab main peeche mudkar dekhta hoon, mujhe afsos nahi hota. Kyunki us dard ne hi mujhe mazboot banaya. Us khamoshi ne hi mujhe khud se milwaya. Aur us tootey hue bharose ne hi mujhe sikhaya ki asli bharosa sabse pehle khud par karna zaroori hai.

Maybe I lost people.
Maybe I lost trust.
Maybe I lost a version of myself.

But somewhere in all that pain,
I found my strength again.

~ Sinu
